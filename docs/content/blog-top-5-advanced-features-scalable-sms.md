- **H3:** Smart Segmentation
- **Paragraph:** Group contacts by behavior, purchase history, location, and preferences so each campaign reaches the right people. Better targeting means fewer opt-outs and more conversions on both promotional and transactional SMS, from flash sales to renewals.
- **Text:** 02
- **H3:** Two-Way Messaging
- **Paragraph:** Let customers reply to confirm appointments, share feedback, or ask for help in the same thread. Two-way SMS turns one-way alerts into conversations, using keywords like YES or STOP, and resolves issues faster.
- **Text:** 03
- **H3:** Automation & Triggers
- **Paragraph:** Automate order confirmations, shipping updates, payment reminders, and win-back messages. Event-based triggers from your CRM send each one at the right moment, with no manual work for your team.
- **Text:** 04
- **H3:** Dynamic Content
- **Paragraph:** Insert fields like customer name, account details, offer codes, and expiry dates into each message. Dynamic templates personalize millions of messages while staying within approved DLT formats and keeping your tone consistent.

- **Paragraph:** Track delivery rates, failures, link clicks, and campaign ROI in real time. Clear delivery reports help marketing and operations teams fine-tune templates, send times, sender IDs, and audience segments.
