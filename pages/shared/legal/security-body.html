Security

At Growtele, protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information is essential to delivering reliable communication services for businesses worldwide. This page describes our general approach to security. It is intended to provide transparency and should be read together with our Privacy Policy and applicable service agreements.

1. Our Commitment to Security

We design and operate our platform with security in mind, recognizing that customers depend on Growtele for business-critical messaging, voice, email, and related communications. We continually work to improve our safeguards as threats, technologies, and business needs evolve.

2. Data Protection

We apply reasonable measures to help protect customer and user information processed through our Services. These measures are intended to reduce risks of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss, consistent with the nature of our services and industry practices.

3. Secure Infrastructure

Our platform relies on infrastructure and hosting arrangements selected to support availability and operational security. We use administrative and technical controls designed to protect systems that store and process service data, including network protections and secure configuration practices appropriate to our environment.

4. Access Controls

Access to production systems and customer data is limited to authorized personnel who require such access to perform their job responsibilities. We support role-based access concepts and review access as part of our operational practices.

5. Monitoring and Incident Response

We monitor our environment for signs of unusual activity and maintain procedures to investigate and respond to potential security incidents. When we identify issues that may affect customers, we work to contain, remediate, and communicate in a manner consistent with the circumstances and our contractual obligations.

6. Secure Development Practices

We incorporate security considerations into the development and maintenance of our platform, including code review, change management, and testing practices suited to our release process. We aim to address vulnerabilities in a timely manner when they are identified.

7. Third-Party Services

Growtele uses carefully selected third-party providers for certain infrastructure, delivery channels, and operational functions. We evaluate vendors according to our business requirements and apply contractual and operational measures designed to protect information shared with them.

8. User Security Responsibilities

Customers and users play an important role in security. You are responsible for safeguarding account credentials, configuring access for your team appropriately, and using the platform in compliance with applicable laws and best practices for your industry. Report suspected unauthorized account activity to us promptly.

9. Vulnerability Reporting / Responsible Disclosure

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability affecting Growtele systems, please report it to us at enquiry@growtele.com with sufficient detail to help us investigate. We ask that you do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities until we have had a reasonable opportunity to review and address them. We appreciate responsible research conducted in good faith.

10. Security Incident Communication

If a security incident materially affects your use of the Services or personal information we process on your behalf, we will provide notifications as required by applicable law and consistent with our agreements with you. Contact your account representative or our support team for service-specific questions.
