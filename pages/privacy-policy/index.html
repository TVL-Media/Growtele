Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Growtele (“Growtele,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you visit our website, use our communication platform and related services, or otherwise interact with us. Growtele operates the Growtele brand and related offerings described on this website, including messaging, voice, email, and related business communication services.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our website or services.

1. Introduction

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently. This policy applies to information collected through our website, customer and partner portals (where available), sales and support channels, and other interactions related to Growtele’s products and services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information such as:

  • Name, business name, job title, and contact details (such as email address and phone number)
  • Account registration and profile information for platform users and administrators
  • Billing and transaction-related information where you purchase or subscribe to services
  • Communications you send to us (for example, support requests, demo inquiries, or feedback)
  • Information you provide when applying for careers, partnerships, or other programs offered on our website

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our website or online services, we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, such as:

  • IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and operating system
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, referring URLs, and general usage patterns
  • Date and time of access and session duration
  • Log and diagnostic data related to service performance and security

2.3 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We and our service providers may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, measure traffic, and improve user experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

3. How We Use Information

We use information we collect for purposes including:

  • Providing, operating, maintaining, and improving our website, platform, and communication services
  • Creating and managing accounts, processing transactions, and delivering customer support
  • Responding to inquiries, demo requests, and other communications
  • Sending service-related notices, administrative messages, and (where permitted) marketing communications
  • Monitoring usage, troubleshooting, analytics, and product development
  • Protecting the security and integrity of our systems, users, and services
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our terms and policies

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers and third parties: With vendors who perform services on our behalf (such as hosting, analytics, customer support tools, payment processing, and communications infrastructure), subject to appropriate contractual safeguards
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law
  • Legal and safety: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect rights, property, safety, and security of Growtele, our users, or others
  • With your direction: When you request or authorize us to share information

5. Service Providers and Third Parties

Our platform may integrate with or rely on third-party services (for example, messaging channels, email delivery, telephony providers, or analytics tools). Those third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review third-party policies when you use integrated features or external links.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and how it is used.

8. User Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal information, or to object to processing or request portability. You may also opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in those messages or contacting us. To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Communications

We may send you transactional or service-related communications related to your account or use of our services. You may also receive marketing or promotional communications where permitted by law and your preferences. You can manage marketing preferences as described above.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing information.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our website and business services are not directed to children under the age of 16 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

12. International Data Transfers

Growtele serves businesses globally. If you access our services from outside your home country, your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate. Where required, we take steps designed to provide appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.
